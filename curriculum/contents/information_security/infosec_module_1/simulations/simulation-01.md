# 🧠 Simulation — Attacker vs Defender

## Scenario

You are responsible for securing a small company system.

### Assets
- Employee credentials
- Customer database
- Internal file server

### Constraints
- Limited budget
- Limited time
- You can only secure **ONE** asset immediately

---

## Your Task

Choose **one** asset to prioritize first.

Think carefully:

- Attackers need only one mistake
- You must assume failure elsewhere

---

## Reflection Prompts

After making your choice, consider:

- Why did you choose this asset?
- What happens if the other assets are compromised?
- Would an attacker target the same asset first?

---

## Key Insight

> Security is not about securing everything.  
> It is about securing **what matters most first**.

There is no single “correct” answer — only **defensible reasoning**.
